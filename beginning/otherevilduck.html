You start your journey to the Evil Duck.

Hearing your pockets rustle, you find a map. It reads, 'Evil Duck Lair' and directions to a cave. With nothing else but a piece of paper, you start your journey alone, the other duckspeople indifferent to your departure.

In the deep forest, you see an odd tablet wedged to the side of a rock. You walk up to it, reading its scripture.

This is your final destination. Find thyself in judgement, shall you know your path.

What the quack does that mean? You think to yourself, maybe you have to recite a specific phrase in order to trigger something.
