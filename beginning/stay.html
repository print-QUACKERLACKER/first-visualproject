The second gate calls to you, and you open the gate.

Standing a few lengths away from you is the Evil Duck. However, instead of attacking you, it watches you blankly. Unnerved, you draw your sword and take a slash at the Evil Duck.

It falls on the ground with a loud thump.

The cold, lonely room is suddenly filled with cheers and quacks. Without your noticing, the duckspeople from the town have followed you into the cave and surrounded you, chanting your name in unison.

Sir Quack the 3rd! Sir Quack the 3rd! Sir Quack the 3rd! Sir Quack the 3rd! Sir Quack the 3rd! Sir Quack the 3rd!
