You waddle to a fruit stand duck who is selling apples. Its tail wags when it sees a customer approach its booth.

Fruit Duck: Greetings, traveler! Are you interested in these fresh, juicy apples? Since you're not from around here, I'll give you a special discount!

The duck hands you a bright red apple splashed with drops of water. You take it and take a quick nibble. It's quacklicious!
