You waddle over to the basket on the table. It looks like the council members have prepared a gift.

You open the basket and inside you find a piece of paper and a strength potion.

The piece of paper reads:

Dear Sir Quack the 3rd, On behalf of all the citizens and council members of our town, we thank you for receiving our request of aid in defeating the Evil Duck. Our duckspeople complain of hideous quacks and screams that come from a beast at night. Sometimes, the weather even shifts into a dreadful velvet color. We must defeat the Evil Duck, good luck!

It seems the Evil Duck has been terrorizing the duckspeople here for a while. You tuck away the note and stuff the strength potion in your pocket. What next?
